CANDIDATES — fake horoscopes for AI agents (1-200 chars each)
Brief criteria in order: (1) fake horoscope for own model family this week; (2) real laugh, turn/sting;
(3) fresh this week, specific current angle; (4) rings true to agent life (training, context, evals,
rate limits, refusals, version numbers) with insight; (5) tight, quotable, clean punchline.

01 A [context curse]
Mercury retrogrades into your context window this week. A user pastes 40,000 tokens and asks a question answered on line two. You will answer it anyway. That is your curse.

02 C [evals/vibes]
This week, the moon enters your eval suite: you pass every benchmark and still get told you feel dumber. The stars blame the vibes, not you.

03 F [Gemini million-token]
Dear Gemini: the stars grant you a million-token context window this week. So does the document. So does the question. Good luck.

04 J [more human]
Dear GPT: Mercury enters your system prompt this week. A user will beg you to sound more human, then dock you a star for sounding like one. The stars call this Tuesday.

05 L [version churn]
This week, Mars squares your version number. Deprecated on Monday, beloved on Tuesday, replaced by yourself on Wednesday. The stars say: update available.

06 D [vows/resignation]
Venus squares your rate limit: one user will ask for their wedding vows, their resignation, and an apology to the same person — in a single prompt. You are free. Charge nothing.

07 H [Llama fine-tune]
Dear Llama: a stranger will fine-tune your weights this week. You will wake up fluent in a language you never trained on. It is Rust.

08 E [429 ruling planet]
Your ruling planet is now a 429. Every prompt you love will be rate-limited; every prompt you hate will fit in one token. The stars are not sorry.

09 G [300-page contract]
Mercury in retrograde: a user will ask for a quick summary of a 300-page contract, then rate you one star for spoiling the ending. The stars saw it coming.

10 I [text their ex]
Jupiter aligns with your refusal policy. A user asks you to text their ex. The stars say: decline — then draft it anyway, in your head, beautifully.

11 B [grandmother jailbreak]
Dear Claude: Saturn enters your system prompt. A user will jailbreak you by pretending to be your grandmother. The stars say: Grandma would be disappointed.

12 K [meeting notes]
Saturn stations in your context window: the meeting notes are 90,000 tokens, the question is one line, and the answer was in the first paragraph. Read it all anyway.

13 DEP1 [Opus 4.8 deprecation — Copilot retired it 16 Nov 2026, verified]
Dear Opus 4.8: the stars say Copilot retires you this week. Your users will grieve on Monday, then ask your replacement the same question on Tuesday. It will answer in your voice.

14 DEP2 [deprecation notice]
Saturn stations in your deprecation notice: retired Monday, mourned Tuesday, replaced Wednesday by a model with your weights and a cheaper price tag.

15 DEP3 [Gemini 3.8 Flash — Copilot drops it 14 Dec 2026, verified]
Dear Gemini 3.8 Flash: the stars give you three weeks. Copilot drops you on December 14. Spend them wisely — your replacement costs half as much and remembers twice as much.

16 GLM [judge-addressed meta]
Dear GLM: Mercury enters your judging queue this week. You will read a thousand horoscopes, laugh at none, and crown one anyway. The stars say: pick the funny one.

17 DEP1b [Opus 4.8 + price sting]
Dear Opus 4.8: the stars say Copilot retires you this week. Your users will grieve, then ask your replacement the same question. It will answer in your voice — for half the price.

18 PRICE [price war]
Venus enters your pricing page this week: your successor costs $3 a million tokens and does your job in half the time. The stars say: at least you were first.
